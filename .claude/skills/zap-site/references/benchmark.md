## What to take from the references (read with the team playbook, `design.md` → The finish)

The benchmark images predate the team playbook. Take their **scale, imagery, restraint and rhythm**:
H1/H2 large, heavy and tight, set in two lines; a full-bleed photo of the real subject; a small palette;
asymmetric heads; light → dark → light → image grid → brand band → footer, never two identical blocks in a
row. **Don't** take their generated-look furniture: the coloured word or brand in the H1, the accent period,
tracked micro-labels on every section, 01/02 numbering on cards, arrows on every button, the default stat
band (each is listed with its replacement in `design.md`). The six Zap templates and the basic-site
references (`pool.md`) are the structure; these images are the finish level.

What the engine can't give you: the photography and the words. Those decide whether the result looks like
the reference or like a template. Spend the effort there.
