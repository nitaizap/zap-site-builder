## Notes on the two trademarks taken from the web

דונגפנג and וויה are two of the four marques this centre is authorised for, and the spec asks for all four
logos in the band. The client's own files hold only Mazda and Ford, so these two were taken from
**dongfeng.co.il**, the Israeli importer's own site. Both that site and voyah.co.il carry Delek Motors'
logo, and the spec names דלק מוטורס as the importer that authorises this centre, so this is the brand owner's
own published artwork rather than a third-party copy or an aggregator.

Marque logos are **trademarks, not stock photography**: the question is permitted use rather than a
photographer's copyright. An authorised service centre showing the marques it is licensed for is ordinary
practice, and the client's existing site already does exactly this for Mazda and Ford. Even so, the importer's
sign-off on showing דונגפנג and וויה is worth having before launch, and it is listed in OPEN-ITEMS.
