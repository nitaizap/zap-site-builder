# מהירות האור שליחויות — pilot (team playbook + template pool)

Test rebuild only. The deployed site (branch `site/mehirut-haor`, live/staging) is untouched; facts, copy, SEO,
keyword map and photos are copied from it as-is (see its NOTES.md for sources). Only the design and the
composition are new.

## Design brief
- **Subject:** documents, cheques, parcels and pallets moved by scooter and van, every day, for offices.
- **Audience and moment:** an office manager at a law/accounting firm with a document that must arrive today.
- **The page's single job:** call / leave details for a courier.

## Direction
- Base template **5** (metal works: white + red, overlay tiles, red gradient) + deviations: the logo's own
  red + yellow (the live site used burgundy + brass), IBM Plex Sans Hebrew as the one family (live: Rubik +
  Assistant), preset `industrial` (live: `signature`, light tone), motion `subtle` (live: lively).
- **The one bold thing:** the moving road (the brief asked for moving vehicles). Removed: hero slider, scooter
  ride, scroll progress bar, marquee, stat band, accent words/dots, eyebrows.
- Home (10 sections): cover hero → about `split:card` → overlay service tiles → road → why us `features:rows`
  → how it works `steps:stack` → clients `features:checks` → reviews over a photo → FAQ beside a photo →
  `cta:photo` on yellow (+ the site-wide lead band).
