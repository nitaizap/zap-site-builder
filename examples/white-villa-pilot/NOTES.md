# הוילה הלבנה ינוב — pilot (team playbook + template pool)

Test rebuild only. The deployed site (branch `site/white-villa`, staging 80392936.ussl.co.il) is untouched;
facts, copy, SEO and the client's own photos are copied from it (see its NOTES.md). Design and composition are new.

## Design brief
- **Subject:** a whitewashed private villa on 3.5 dunam in moshav Yanuv: pool with a slide, orchard, lawn, jacaranda.
- **Audience and moment:** a family planning a weekend together, or hosts planning a brit / henna for up to 150.
- **The page's single job:** check a free date (call / leave details).

## Direction
- Base template **2** (restaurant/events: beige surfaces, rounded, inset hero, staggered photos) + deviations:
  olive + jacaranda violet from the garden (live: pool teal + coral), Miriam Libre as the one family (live: Suez
  One + Assistant), preset `clean`, motion `subtle`; no garland, no carousels, no stat band, no accent words.
- **The one bold thing:** the inset hero carrying the client's own pool video.
- Home (8 sections + lead band): inset video hero → `split:duo` → `gallery:stagger` → two ways (`cards:framed`)
  → amenities `features:rows` → `tiles` (pool / orchard / table) → FAQ beside the jacaranda → `cta:photo`.
